Voidwise

Openings for MEP crossings in Revit

What it does

Voidwise finds where ducts, pipes and cable trays cross the walls or floors of a linked architectural / structural model, plans one opening for each crossing, lets you review every opening in 3D, and places the approved ones with the add-in's opening families. Works with Revit 2024, 2025 and 2026.

Where to find it

Two places in Revit: the Voidwise tab (Wall Openings, Slab Openings, Export Openings, Import Openings) and the Voidwise panel on the Add-Ins tab (same four commands in one menu). Press F1 on a button to open this page.

Quick start - Wall Openings / Slab Openings

  1. Connect the model. Pick the loaded Revit link that holds the walls (or floors).
  2. Options. Choose what to check (ducts, pipes, cable trays), the clearances, and the processing scope: the whole model, the active view, or Selection (the window hides so you can pick elements in Revit, then returns with the picked counts).
  3. Review. Each planned opening is shown in 3D. Approve, reject, or flag it as Needs Review. Neighbouring elements crossing the same wall can share one rectangular opening.
  4. Create. Only approved openings (and, if you tick it, Pending ones) are placed. Rejected and Needs Review openings are never created.

Sending openings to the architect or structural engineer

Export Openings writes a .voidwise file (shared coordinates, position, size, orientation, family) and a CSV list. The other discipline opens their model and runs Import Openings: Voidwise finds the wall or floor at each opening, places the family and cuts the host. Importing the same file again updates changed openings. Importing is free for everyone.

Plans and trial

A free trial gives the full product for a limited time. After that, Basic covers wall openings and Pro adds slab openings. Licensing is checked online through your Autodesk account.

Settings

Settings page: dark / light theme, window size (90 / 100 / 110 %), language (English, Arabic, German), save your option defaults or restore factory defaults.

Good practice

  • You remain responsible for checking every opening against your project requirements before issuing drawings or models.
  • Work in a saved model and review the Summary page after creating.
  • If a family cannot be loaded or a wall is not found, the Result card lists the problems.
