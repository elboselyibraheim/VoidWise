Who we are

Ibraheimelbosely ("we") publishes Voidwise. Contact: nawasolutionmail@gmail.com.

What Voidwise sends to our licence server

Each time Voidwise starts (and at most every few minutes after that), it sends the following to our licence server over HTTPS, only to check that you may use the Software:

  • your Autodesk account ID and Autodesk user name (from Revit);
  • an anonymous device ID - a one-way hash of the Windows machine ID and Windows user, which cannot be turned back into a name;
  • the Voidwise version and the Revit version;
  • the time of use.

We never receive your models, element data, file names, project names, or any other project information.

What we store and why

On the licence server we keep: your Autodesk account ID, user name, the anonymous device IDs, first and last use dates, number of checks, the Voidwise and Revit versions last used, your trial start date and plan/licence period. We use this to run the trial, check licences, prevent misuse, and support you. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

What stays on your computer

In %APPDATA%\Voidwise: your settings (theme, size, language, saved options), a short log of the last run (last-run.txt), whether you accepted the terms, and a counter used to decide when to ask for a rating. None of this is sent to us.

Ratings and feedback

The rating button opens our page on the Autodesk App Store in your browser. The feedback button opens your email program; nothing is sent unless you send the email.

Sharing with third parties

We share the licence data above only with the service provider that hosts our licence server (PythonAnywhere) and, for purchases, with Autodesk. We use no analytics tools, advertising networks or third-party SDKs in Voidwise, and we have no parent, subsidiary or related company that receives this data. Any third party that processes this data for us must protect it at least as strongly as this policy states, and may use it only to provide the service to us.

Retention

We keep licence records while your account is active and for up to 24 months after your last use, then delete or anonymise them. Ask us to delete your records at any time.

Withdrawing consent and deleting your data

You can withdraw your consent at any time: stop using Voidwise and uninstall it (Windows Settings > Apps, or delete the Voidwise.bundle folder), and write to nawasolutionmail@gmail.com asking us to delete your licence records. We delete or anonymise them, normally within 30 days, and confirm by email. Without the licence check Voidwise cannot start, because the check is how the trial and your licence work.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by writing to nawasolutionmail@gmail.com. If you are in the EEA or UK you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Where data is processed

The licence server is hosted by PythonAnywhere in the United States. Autodesk may also process purchase information under its own privacy statement.

Changes

If we change what is collected, we will update this page and the Software's notice before the change applies.
